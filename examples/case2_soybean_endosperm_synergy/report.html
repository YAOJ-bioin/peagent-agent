How the agent worked

  1. Loaded the motif-synergy procedure and found the pairs already reported in the paper's Table S4, so it framed the work as a re-test.
  2. Took the motifs from the paper's lexicon: HD-ZIP CATTAATT and three TCP patterns found in soybean endosperm.
  3. Ran the paper's protocol for each pair: all 6 endosperm groups pooled (8,511 cells), 100 GC-matched genomic backgrounds, spacings 0-200 bp, four orientations.
  4. Sent a literature sub-agent to look for experimental evidence of an HD-ZIP-TCP interaction.
  5. Sent a verifier sub-agent to re-check every number against the run records, and corrected the report.
